A SPACE FOR YOU

You deserve a space where you can be heard.

Life can sometimes feel heavier than we expected. You may be navigating anxiety, emotional overwhelm, relationship difficulties, grief, burnout, trauma, major life changes, or simply feeling stuck and unsure of what comes next.

Sometimes, you may not even know exactly what is wrong — you just know that something doesn't feel right. Counselling offers a space to pause, reflect and make sense of what you're carrying, without judgement.

At Lily & Light, your story matters. I believe meaningful counselling begins with empathy, respect, genuine listening and a therapeutic relationship where you can feel safe enough to be yourself.

You don't have to carry everything alone.

MY APPROACH

Gentle. Collaborative. Individualised.

Therapy is a relationship, not a formula. I begin by understanding you — your experiences, concerns, strengths and what you hope to change.

Listen

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

If your question isn't answered here, you're welcome to reach out.

What is the duration of a session?

Individual counselling sessions are 60 minutes, while couples counselling sessions are 90 minutes.

How much is a counselling session?

Individual counselling is RM250 per session. Couples counselling is RM350 per session.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Online counselling is available via Google Meet for clients throughout Malaysia.

Where are physical sessions conducted?

In-person counselling is available in Bandar Sri Sendayan, Seremban, and Petaling Jaya.

Do I need to know what I want to talk about before therapy?

No. You don't need to have everything figured out before reaching out. We can begin by exploring what is currently happening and what brought you to counselling.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is confidential within the applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements. The limits of confidentiality will be explained clearly during the counselling process and informed consent.

What if I'm not sure whether therapy is right for me?

That's completely okay. You can reach out with your questions first. We can discuss what you're looking for and whether counselling may be appropriate for your needs.
