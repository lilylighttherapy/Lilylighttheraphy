Privacy Policy

This website is operated by Lily & Light. Information submitted through the enquiry form is used to respond to counselling enquiries and appointment requests.

The enquiry form opens WhatsApp on your device. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive or confidential personal information through the website or WhatsApp enquiry message. Detailed clinical information can be discussed through the appropriate counselling process.

Lily & Light aims to handle personal information responsibly and only for purposes connected with your enquiry and counselling services. Third-party services you choose to use, such as WhatsApp or Google Meet, are also subject to their own privacy terms.

If you have questions about how your information is handled, please contact Lily & Light through the contact details on the main website.
